# Sources — Niki&Niki Bakery demo

Facts used on the unofficial demo page, checked 15 November 2026 (Europe/Madrid). Menu items and prices without a first-party source are left as placeholders (`[producto]`, `[bebida]`, `[precio]`).

## Identity and address

- Name **Niki&Niki Bakery**, address **Avinguda / Avenida de la Paz, 4, 03724 Moraira**:
  - [Pueblito](https://pueblito.es/moraira/niki-niki-bakery)
  - [Hello Jávea](https://hellojavea.com/es/niki-niki)
  - [gdweb.es](https://www.gdweb.es/empresas/niki-niki-bakery-moraira)

Hello Jávea describes this shop as the Moraira branch of the Dénia bakery, open since autumn 2024.

## Contact

- Instagram **@nikiniki.bakery** is the “Sitio web” / “página web” field on Pueblito, Hello Jávea and gdweb.
- Tel. **+34 625 02 63 00** appears on Pueblito and gdweb. The demo uses it for a telephone link and a WhatsApp button.

## What they serve (not a full menu)

- Hello Jávea mentions sourdough bread, pastry with French butter, specialty coffee and brunch, and names cinnamon rolls, cardamom buns, Turkish eggs, banana bread, carrot cake and a pastrami sandwich (from a visit write-up).
- No official first-party menu or prices were found. The three cards on the demo are placeholders; the Hello Jávea names are only in the note, not presented as a confirmed priced menu.

## Hours

Pueblito and gdweb agree: Monday and Tuesday 8:00–15:30; Wednesday to Sunday 8:00–22:00. Those hours are shown on the demo with a note that they come from public directories and should be confirmed, because they are not from a first-party website.

## Map

The pin at **38.6881249, 0.1321664** is approximate, on OpenStreetMap’s **Avinguda de la Pau** (the Valencian name of Avenida de la Paz) in central Moraira. OSM has the street but, as of 15 November 2026, no surveyed bakery node and no housenumber 4. The page caption states this.
