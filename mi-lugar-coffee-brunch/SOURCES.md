## Not used as confirmed facts (placeholders or omitted)

- **Hours.** Public listings disagree on which day is closed (one shows Monday closed and Tuesday 8:00–16:00; an aggregator shows the opposite). The demo uses `[horario]`.
  - https://verlacarta.es/cafeteria/mi-lugar-coffee-brunch-x-bia-alicante
  - https://exa.ai/library/place/v1kd1qqxgb4
- **Menu and prices.** The third-party menu page has no actual menu (“The menu is not available yet”). Marketing copy on that page (for example homemade sandwiches) was not treated as an official carta. The demo uses carta placeholders.
- **Instagram.** No public Instagram for this Jávea café was confirmed. A search hit for @milugar._ was a generic quote account, not this business.
- **Email** milugarcafebrunch@gmail.com and a numeric Facebook id appeared only on an aggregator (https://exa.ai/library/place/v1kd1qqxgb4) and were not independently confirmed on a page we opened. Not used on the demo.
- **Postcode.** The two fetched listings use 03730; OpenStreetMap labels number 5 as 03738. The demo gives the area (Platja de l’Arenal) without choosing one code.
