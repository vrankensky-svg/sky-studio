# Sources — Woody's demo

Facts used on the unofficial demo page, checked 15 November 2026 (Europe/Madrid). Anything not confirmed across public sources is left as a placeholder (`[plato]`, `[bebida]`, `[precio]`, `[horario]`, `[teléfono]`).

## Identity and address

- Name **Woody's** / **Woody's Pretty Healthy Kitchen**.
- Address **Av. del Arenal, 18**, 03730 Xàbia, from [Comercios Comunitat Valenciana](https://www.comercioscomunitatvalenciana.com/es/comercios/view/woodys).
- **Edificio Ancora, local B** is added by [Hello Jávea](https://hellojavea.com/es/woodys).
- [Guía Repsol — Woody's (Solete, Cafeterías)](https://www.guiarepsol.com/es/fichas/solete/woody-s-334187/) gives Av. Arenal, 03730 Xàbia, without the building name.

## Contact

- Instagram **@woodys.javea**: Guía Repsol (“Ver Instagram”). A 2021 [Javea Connect](https://www.javeaconnect.co.uk/business-of-the-week-woodys-javea/) article mentioned @woodys.es; the demo uses the current Repsol handle.
- Facebook [facebook.com/woodys.javea](https://www.facebook.com/woodys.javea) is the “Web” field on the Comercios listing and on an [exa.ai Google-data mirror](https://exa.ai/library/place/81mzs0fgvfp). The demo links Instagram, not Facebook.
- Phone numbers **conflict**: 673 643 802 (Comercios, exa) versus 673 643 892 (Guía Repsol). The demo shows `[teléfono]` and has no call or WhatsApp button.

## What they serve (not a full menu)

- Guía Repsol describes colourful breakfasts, coffee, açaí bowls, salads, matcha, smoothies and fresh juices, with gluten-free and vegan options.
- Hello Jávea also mentions poke and a smoked-salmon toast.
- No official first-party menu or prices were found. Menu rows on the demo are placeholders.

## Hours

Guía Repsol does not publish a usable timetable on the listing. A third-party Google-data mirror lists hours, but they were not treated as confirmed. The demo leaves hours as `[horario]`.

## Map

The pin at **38.7718006, 0.1873771** is the OpenStreetMap node “Woody's” (amenity=restaurant) on Avinguda de l'Arenal, Xàbia. OSM has no housenumber; the page caption states this and keeps the listed address (18, Edificio Ancora, local B).

## Not used

- The conflicting phone numbers, except as a reason to leave the number unconfirmed.
- Aggregator hours and review scores.
- The older Instagram handle @woodys.es, except as a note in this file.
