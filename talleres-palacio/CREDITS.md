## Map

`assets/images/map.jpg` was composed from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) raster tiles (© OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)). The marker at 38.78068, 0.152527 is approximate for the publicly listed address Av. de Gata, 46, Xàbia; OpenStreetMap has no surveyed workshop entrance and tags that stretch as CV-7343 (el Tossal). The interactive map iframe also uses OpenStreetMap.
