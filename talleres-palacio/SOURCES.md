# Sources — Talleres Palacio demo

Facts used on the unofficial demo page, checked 15 November 2026 (Europe/Madrid). Anything not confirmed across public sources is left as a placeholder on the page (`[horario]`, `[servicios]`, `[precio]`, and `[teléfono]` for the tow number).

## Identity and address

- Name **Talleres Palacio**, address **Av. de Gata, 46, 03730 Xàbia / Jávea**, and landline **965 79 39 85** (+34 965 79 39 85):
  - [Portal Turístico de Xàbia — Talleres Palacio](https://www.xabia.org/ver/2850/Talleres-Palacios.html)
  - [Comercios Comunitat Valenciana — Talleres Palacio](https://comercioscomunitatvalenciana.com/es/comercios/view/talleres-palacio)

Some other directories list the same landline and a similar address form (for example “Ctra. Gata, 46”). The demo uses the wording from the municipal and regional listings.

## Services

- **Mecánica, electricidad, chapa, pintura** and a **servicio de grúa** appear on the municipal listing above. The same four workshop services also appear on the Comunitat Valenciana listing, which additionally describes the business as a **taller multimarca** and lists **electromecánica, neumáticos, aire acondicionado, pre-ITV** and work with insurers. Those extra items are shown on the demo with a note that they come from that listing and should be confirmed.

## Grúa / 24 hours / mobile numbers

- The municipal listing says “Servicio de Grúa 24h. 608 86 23 33”.
- The Comunitat Valenciana listing gives a different mobile, **609 434 338**, and does not label it as a 24-hour tow line.

Because those numbers conflict, the demo does not put a “Grúa 24h” call button or either mobile on the page. The tow phone is left as `[teléfono]`. The landline, which both listings share, is used for the plain call button. Opening hours, including any 24-hour tow availability, are not treated as confirmed.

## Email and domain

- Email **administracion@talleresygruaspalacio.com** is on the Comunitat Valenciana listing.
- The domain **talleresygruaspalacio.com** is registered at DonDominio but, as of 15 November 2026, only served a parking page (HTTPS used a `*.dondominio.com` certificate and “Dominio no encontrado”). The demo does not present itself as that domain or as an official site.

## Hours

Opening hours are not confirmed from a first-party source. Third-party aggregators publish hours; they are not used on the demo.

## Map

The map pin at **38.78068, 0.152527** is an approximate location in the Av. de Gata / CV-734 area of Xàbia (reverse geocode: CV-7343, el Tossal). Nominatim/OpenStreetMap did not return a surveyed workshop node or the street name “Avenida de Gata”. The page caption states this.

## Facebook

A Google-data mirror lists [facebook.com/1440002862700121](https://www.facebook.com/1440002862700121) for a listing at this address and landline (under the personal name Juan Francisco Palacio Tormos). A fetch of that URL on 15 November 2026 was redirected to Facebook login, so the page title could not be confirmed independently. It is not linked from the demo.

- Hours, ratings and reviews from aggregator listings.
- The conflicting mobile numbers, except as a reason to leave the tow phone unconfirmed.
